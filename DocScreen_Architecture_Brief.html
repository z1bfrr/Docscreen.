DocScreen — Architecture Brief

Multi-Layer AI Document Screening & Decision Fusion Pipeline

SIH Prototype Reference

1. Workflow Execution Summary

1. Ingestion & Image Preprocessing

  • SHA-256 Hashing: Instant cryptographic duplicate detection.
  • Quality Scoring: Laplacian blur and luminosity histogram analysis.
  • Contrast Normalization: Adaptive CLAHE preprocessing.

2. Dual OCR & Structured Extraction

  • Dual-Engine Pipeline: Tesseract primary with EasyOCR fallback.
  • Spatial Extraction: Word-level bounding boxes and confidence metrics.
  • Regex Anchors: Extraction of Name, DOB, and ID Numbers.

3. Vision Forensics & Tamper Detection

  • Error Level Analysis (ELA): Identifies compression history anomalies from digital splices at Q=90.
  • Noise Variance Grid: 12x12 patch analysis to detect pasted elements.
  • Edge Disparity: Spots synthetic crop boundaries.

4. Consistency & ML Outlier Detection

  • QR Cross-Validation: Pyzbar decodes QR payload and verifies consistency against printed OCR text.
  • Temporal Logic: Validates Issue < Expiry and age-DOB checks.
  • Isolation Forest: Flags multidimensional outlier vectors.

Technical Specifications

2. Signal Fusion Risk Engine

The system synthesizes all detection outputs into a single index (0 – 100) using a weighted linear model:

Signal Weight Target Evaluation
Visual Tamper 25% ELA compression energy & noise variance inconsistencies
Template & Layout 15% Shifted bounding boxes, missing official emblems, or margin modifications
Cross-Field Consistency 15% QR code payload discrepancies vs printed OCR document text
ML Anomaly (Isolation Forest) 15% Multidimensional outlier features compared to standard distributions
OCR Anomalies 10% Character recognition confidence drops and distorted letterforms
Pattern Violations 10% Checksum/regex failures (Aadhaar, PAN, Passport specifications)
Metadata & Quality 10% EXIF software modification markers, blur, and glare penalties

3. Decision Classification & Overrides

LOW RISK (0 – 30)
Standard verification; eligible for automated processing
MEDIUM RISK (31 – 65)
Borderline confidence; queued for manual officer review
HIGH RISK (66 – 100)
High likelihood of digital tampering or data contradiction

Critical Finding Override

If any check identifies an unambiguous contradiction (e.g., QR code payload disagrees with printed text or photo splice detected), the overall score is automatically elevated to ≥ 70 (HIGH RISK) regardless of other passing signals.
